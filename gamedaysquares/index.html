Common questions

How do I set up a board? Create a game with your own team names and colors, then tap a square to add a player, or open Players to allocate counts or fill the board randomly. Randomize the numbers when everyone is in, then enter each quarter's score under Scores to see the winning square.

Where is my data stored? Entirely on your device. There is no account, no backend and nothing is uploaded to us. See the privacy policy.

Can I use real money or prizes? No. Game Day Squares does not support wagers, deposits, payments or payouts. Use non-cash reward labels such as "Pizza" or "Bragging Rights" instead.

Is this affiliated with the NFL or any team? No. Game Day Squares is an independent, unofficial party game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any professional football league, team or player.

How do I remove ads? The free edition shows ads. Open Settings → Game Day Squares Pro to make a one-time purchase that removes ads and unlocks unlimited players and expanded exports, or tap Restore Purchases if you've already bought it on this account.

My numbers or scores look wrong. Numbers lock once assigned, and each axis contains 0–9 exactly once. Winners are calculated from the last digit of each entered cumulative score. Double-check the board before announcing results, and email us if something still looks off.

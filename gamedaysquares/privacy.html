Privacy Policy

Game Day Squares · effective September 19, 2026

The short version: your game night belongs to you. Game Day Squares has no account and no backend of its own. The free edition shows ads served by Google AdMob to keep the app free; going Pro removes them. Nothing you enter into the game is ever sent to us.

What we store on your device

Names you enter, teams, square assignments, round scores and your preferences, kept in the app's private storage on your device. No account is required, and none of it is transmitted to us.

Advertising

The free edition shows banner and interstitial ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and approximate location to select and measure ads. On iOS we ask permission through App Tracking Transparency before that identifier is read — decline and every feature still works, you'll just see less relevant ads. You can reset or limit ad personalization from your device's own privacy settings at any time. See Google's advertising policy.

In-app purchase

Game Day Squares Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase that removes ads and unlocks unlimited players and expanded exports. It is billed and processed by the App Store or Google Play — we never receive or store your payment details, and the platform can restore your purchase to another device signed into the same account.

Sharing

Sharing is optional. If you choose Share, the board or winner image may contain player and team names. Your device's share sheet sends that image only to the app or person you select; their own privacy practices apply from that point on.

Permissions

No camera, contacts, location, microphone or photo-library permission is required for gameplay. Sound and vibration are optional and can be turned off in Settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual games from their menu at any time. Uninstalling the app removes local app data, subject to operating-system behavior. Android backup is disabled for this app. iOS device backups may include app data according to your device's own backup settings.

Third parties

We do not sell your data. Google AdMob (ads) and the App Store or Google Play (billing for Pro) are the only third parties this app talks to — it runs no server of its own and includes no analytics SDK.

Children

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

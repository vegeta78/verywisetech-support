Privacy Policy

Game Day Squares · effective September 15, 2026

The short version: your game night belongs to you. Game Day Squares has no account, no backend, no analytics, no advertising SDK, no sports service and no AI service. Nothing you enter is sent to us.

What we store on your device

Names you enter, teams, square assignments, round scores and your preferences, kept in the app's private storage on your device. No account is required, and none of it is transmitted to us.

Sharing

Sharing is optional. If you choose Share, the board or winner image may contain player and team names. Your device's share sheet sends that image only to the app or person you select; their own privacy practices apply from that point on.

Permissions

No camera, contacts, location, microphone or photo-library permission is required. Sound and vibration are optional and can be turned off in Settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual games from their menu at any time. Uninstalling the app removes local app data, subject to operating-system behavior. Android backup is disabled for this app. iOS device backups may include app data according to your device's own backup settings.

Third parties

We do not sell your data. This app includes no analytics or advertising SDK of any kind, and talks to no server of ours or anyone else's.

Children

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because the app collects no personal data at all, there is nothing for us to hold.

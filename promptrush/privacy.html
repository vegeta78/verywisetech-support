PromptRush — Privacy Policy Last updated: 15 September 2026 PromptRush is a prompt discovery app. It shows you ready-made ideas for AI photo and video prompts, lets you copy them, and hands you off to the AI app of your choice. 1. What PromptRush is not These are facts about how the app is built, not just promises: No account. No sign-up, no login, no profile, no password. No photo access. The app requests no camera permission and no storage permission. Its only Android permissions are INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and AD_ID. It is not technically capable of reading your photos. No uploads. We have no server that receives content from you. No AI processing by us. PromptRush does not generate images or video and has no inference backend. The generating happens in whichever AI app you choose, on your account and your credits. Prompt images ship inside the app, so browsing the feed contacts no image host. 2. What stays on your device Stored with your device's standard app storage, never transmitted to us, and removed entirely when you uninstall: What Why Saved prompts and when you saved them So Saved works Your edited versions of prompts So your tuned prompt is there next time How many times you copied or tried each prompt The “Tried 3×” label Recent searches Suggestions; clearable in Settings Theme preference Light, dark or system A cached copy of the prompt catalog So the app works offline Ad frequency counters and any ad-free period you earned To pace ads and honour the reward First-launch date, launch count, whether we have asked you to rate the app So the review request is asked once, not repeatedly None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. 3. What is collected Analytics — Google Firebase Analytics Anonymous, aggregated usage events, so we know which prompts are worth expanding: app opens, prompt views, copies, saves, shares, category opens, searches, “Surprise Me” usage, which AI tool you chose, and whether an ad or review request was shown. Prompt events carry the prompt's id, category and type. Searches record only the number of characters you typed — never the text. We never set a user ID or any user property. Firebase Analytics also collects standard device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, country-level location derived from your IP, and a resettable app-instance identifier). See Firebase privacy. Crash reporting — Firebase Crashlytics If the app crashes we receive the stack trace plus device and OS details, so we can fix it. No personal content is attached. Remote configuration — Firebase Remote Config The app downloads settings — the prompt catalog address and the advertising switches. This is a download; it sends no personal data about you. Advertising — Google AdMob The app shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. Google may use your device's advertising identifier and approximate (country-level) location to serve and measure ads. You can control this: Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete or reset advertising ID iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking See Google advertising policies. Rewarded ads are always optional. Watching one to remove ads for 24 hours is a choice; declining costs you no feature. Every prompt in the app is available either way. In-app review — Google Play / Apple The app may ask you to rate it using the operating system's own review sheet. We receive no data from it — not whether you rated, nor what you said. We store only the date we asked, on your device, so we do not ask again. 4. When you open an AI tool Tapping ChatGPT, Gemini or another tool does two things: Copies the prompt to your clipboard. Opens that service. For services that support it, the prompt text is included in the web address so their composer is pre-filled. That means the prompt text — including any edits you made — is sent to that service as part of opening it. Once you leave PromptRush, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply. We send them nothing about you: no identifier, no analytics, no profile. You attach your own photo there, in your own account. 5. Children PromptRush is directed at adults (18+) and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The third-party AI services the app links to set their own minimum ages, which you should check. 6. Your choices Clear local data: Settings → Clear search history, or uninstall the app. Opt out of personalised ads: use the operating-system controls above. Remove ads temporarily: Settings → Remove ads for 24 hours. Stop all collection: uninstall the app. Because we hold no account and no identifier that points at you as a person, there is no profile for us to look up, export or delete on request. If you have a question about your data, write to us at the address below. 7. Data sharing and transfers We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone other than the Google services named above, which process it as described in their own policies. Those services may process data outside your country, including in the United States. 8.

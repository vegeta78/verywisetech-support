PromptRush Support

AI photo & video prompt discovery.

Need help or have a suggestion?

Email verywisetech@gmail.com. For a bug, include your device model, operating-system version and app version. Please do not send passwords or sensitive photos.

How do I use a prompt?

Browse an idea, tap Try This, then copy or edit the prompt. Open your chosen AI service and attach a photo there if requested. PromptRush provides prompts; the external service generates the result.

Where are my saved prompts?

Open the Saved tab. Favourites and saved edits are stored on your device without an account. They are not synced by PromptRush between devices.

Can I browse offline?

Bundled prompts and covers are available offline. Remote catalog updates and external AI tools need an internet connection.

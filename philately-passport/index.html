Common questions

Where is my data stored? Entirely on your device, encrypted. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded to us. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up my passport? Settings → Export My Passport writes your records and photographs to a file you can save anywhere.

Is this an official India Post app? No. Philately Passport is an independent, unofficial companion app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by India Post. Records you create are personal collection records only and do not constitute official verification.

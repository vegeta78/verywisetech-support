How to Play

  • Tap Draw a black card to reveal a prompt card for the round.
  • Players answer using their own physical (or another app's) white cards.
  • Use Players & scoring to track who's winning across rounds.
  • Add your own prompts any time from Custom cards.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are the white (answer) cards?
Black Cards Mega Pack deals prompt (black) cards only — pair it with your own physical deck or another answer-card app.

My custom cards disappeared or the app crashed.
Please email us at verywisetech@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version so we can look into it.

How do I remove ads?
Use the "Remove Ads" option in the app to purchase an ad-free experience.

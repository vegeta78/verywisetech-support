Common questions

How do I make music?

Choose an instrument and color, draw on the canvas, then tap Play. Horizontal position controls when a note plays in the loop; higher lines produce higher notes. Strokes from different instruments can play together.

What do Scale and Harmony change?

Scale changes the notes available to your sketch. Harmony adds notes around each drawn note; Single note preserves the simplest playback. You can also change the key and tempo.

How do I edit a sketch?

Use Erase on the canvas, Undo or Redo for recent edits, Echo to repeat strokes later in the loop, Flip to reverse their timing, or Clear to start over.

Where are my saved sketches?

Tap Save to keep the current sketch on this device, and Open to restore it. The app does not sync sketches across devices. Saving a new sketch replaces the previous locally saved sketch.

Recording does not work on my device.

Recording depends on the audio and video features available in your device's web view. If Record shows an error or the saved video lacks audio, email us with your device and iOS version so we can investigate.

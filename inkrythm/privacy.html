PRIVACY POLICY

Your sketches stay on your device.

Effective 26 September 2026. This policy describes the Inkrythm mobile app published by Very Wise Tech.

What the app stores

When you tap Save, Inkrythm stores your sketch strokes and music settings in the app's local storage on your device. Open reads that local copy. A new save replaces the previous saved sketch. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data may remove the sketch.

When recording is supported and you choose to record, the app creates a video of the canvas with its internally generated audio and offers it for saving on your device. The recording is not uploaded to Very Wise Tech by the app.

Data collection and sharing

The current Inkrythm app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or an in-app service that sends your sketches or recordings to Very Wise Tech. The app does not request microphone or camera input to generate its music or recordings.

If you choose to email support, we receive the information you put in your message and use it to respond and investigate your request. Your email provider's privacy practices also apply. We do not sell your support messages.

Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, payment, or diagnostic information under Apple's own privacy policy. That processing is separate from the app's local sketch storage.

Your choices

You can replace a saved sketch by saving another one, or remove locally stored app data by deleting the app. You control whether to record, save, or share a video outside Inkrythm. To ask about a support email you sent us, contact us at the address below.

Children

Inkrythm does not ask users to create profiles and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above will be revised when we publish an update.

Questions about privacy? Email verywisetech@gmail.com.

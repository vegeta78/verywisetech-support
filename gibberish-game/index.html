How to Play

  • Best with teams of two or more. Each puzzle is a string of unrelated words that, read aloud, sound like a familiar phrase, name, or place.
  • Pick Easy, Classic, or Hard on the home screen for shorter or trickier phrases.
  • Flip on Teams A & B to keep score — tap the team that got it right to award a point.
  • Say the gibberish out loud, then tap to reveal the answer.
  • Use Share this one to send a phrase to a friend.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does scoring work?
Turn on Teams A & B from the home screen. During a round, tap the team that guessed correctly to award them a point. Reset scores any time from the home screen.

What's the difference between Easy, Classic, and Hard?
Easy uses shorter, more familiar phrases; Hard uses longer or trickier ones. Pick whichever fits your group.

The app crashed or a puzzle looks wrong.
Please email us at verywisetech@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, and the phrase you saw so we can look into it.

How do I remove ads?
Use the "Remove Ads" option on the home screen to purchase an ad-free experience.

I already bought Remove Ads — how do I get it back on a new device?
Open "Remove Ads" from the home screen and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed into the same Apple ID.
